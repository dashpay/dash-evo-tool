Information architecture: before and after

  • Today one DPNS tool under Tools mixes "My usernames" with voting. After: names live with the identity; voting lives with the nodes.
  • Decided (Q1): Masternodes opens on Votes when anything needs a decision; a top-bar chip carries the signal to every screen. Changes #901 VOTE-FR-001 (spec §4).

Username rules
  • Between 3 and 63 characters.
  • Letters, numbers, and hyphens. No hyphen at the start or end.
  • Capital letters are treated as lowercase.
  • Names shorter than 20 characters that use only letters, hyphens, 0, and 1 need a community vote. Adding a digit from 2 to 9 avoids the vote.
