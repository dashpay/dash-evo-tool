Send screen redesign — wireframes

Dash Evo Tool, 2026-10-23. HTML rendering of the ASCII frames in 03-wireframes.md; rules in 02-ux-spec.md, requirements in 01-requirements.md. Today's Send screen asks people to understand Core, Platform, Identity and Shielded before it knows who they are paying, and runs two separate forms (Simple and "Advanced Options"). The redesign is one form with two sections, From and To. Auto is the default way to pick the balance: once the recipient is known, the app picks the cheapest balance that can pay and says what it chose in a plain sentence. Paying your own wallets is its own mode (My wallets), so nobody copies their own address. Advanced becomes an Expert-view option inside each section, and every fee fact lives in one Summary card. Same visual system as the 2026-10-01 usernames canvas. Chips, switches, Max, checkboxes, Fee options and Details are live: click, hover (chip tooltips) and Tab through them. Narrow the window below 640 px to see labels move above their controls (spec §13).

Example data, the same throughout: wallet Main: Core 0.8123, Platform 0.3000, Shielded 0.1000, identity alice 0.2000 (total 1.4123 DASH). Wallet Savings: Core 2.0000, identity carol 0.0500. Network: Testnet.
